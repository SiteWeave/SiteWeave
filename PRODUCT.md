# SiteWeave — Product Context

## Register

**Product** (B2B construction project management). Design serves field and office workflows, not marketing pages.

## Product Purpose

SiteWeave helps construction companies and project teams manage jobs from office and field: projects, phased tasks, schedules, field issues, team communication, calendars, progress reports, and org administration.

## Users

- **General contractors / PMs** — office dashboard, reporting, team setup
- **Supers and field staff** — mobile My Day, task updates, photos, calendar
- **Guest collaborators / subs** — limited project access via invites

## Strategic Principles

- Trust and clarity over playfulness; construction stakes are high
- Field-first mobile: fast loads, large touch targets, offline-tolerant patterns
- Multi-tenant orgs with RBAC; guest access is common
- English and Spanish (i18n)

## Anti-references

- Crypto-wallet playfulness, mascots, gamification points
- Generic SaaS purple-gradient marketing aesthetic
- Modal-heavy flows when inline or bottom-sheet suffices on mobile

## Tone

Professional, supportive, direct. Encourage progress without condescension. No em dashes in UI copy.
